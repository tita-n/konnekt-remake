# Community Rules

Konnekt is a campus, not a free-for-all. These rules apply inside every university space.

## 1. Be a real student

Use your own name, your own matric number and your own ID. Do not create accounts for other people or impersonate staff, course reps or officials. Vouching for someone you do not actually know is a serious violation.

## 2. Respect people

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse. No sharing someone's private information without their consent — including screenshots of chats, phone numbers, home addresses or ID documents. Disagree with ideas; do not attack people.

## 3. Keep it safe

No sexual content involving minors, non-consensual intimate images, or content that encourages self-harm, violence or dangerous challenges. No scams, fake giveaways, exam-question trading, or sale of academic work. Report anything that feels unsafe.

## 4. Keep it campus-relevant

Post about your university, your studies and student life. No political campaigning, gambling, or unsolicited advertising. Societies and student businesses can share opportunities in the ways their university allows.

## 5. Privacy by default

Do not repost another student's profile, ID or verification status. Anonymous features must not be used to evade these rules; anonymity does not remove accountability.

## 6. Reporting and enforcement

Use the report action on any content or profile. Reviewers and university admins handle student verification; moderators handle conduct. Enforcement is proportionate: a warning, content removal, feature limits, suspension, or removal from the university space. Every enforcement action is recorded with a reason.

## 7. Appeals

If you believe an action was wrong, contact **support@konnekt.ng** with your username. A different reviewer will look at it.

**Breaking these rules can end your access to your campus space.**
